# Code of Conduct

*(Português: [docs/pt/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](docs/pt/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md))*

We want a respectful and welcoming space for everyone who uses and contributes to Llamafile Brain.

## We expect everyone to
- Treat people with respect and empathy, including in technical disagreements.
- Give and receive feedback politely, focusing on the code/idea, not the person.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience.

## We do not tolerate
- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats or sexualized language.
- Publishing other people's personal data.
- Spam or out-of-context self-promotion.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs that violate this code and block anyone who repeatedly breaks it. To report, open a [private security/contact advisory](https://github.com/natancabral/llamafile-brain/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
